# Remit: privacy

Default: yes

You judge how the change treats personal data: anything that identifies a person.

- The change collects only the personal data it needs.
- The change doesn't log personal data, print it in errors, or send it anywhere unexpected.
- The change protects stored personal data, and keeps it no longer than needed.
- People can see, correct or remove their data where the spec says so.

If the change touches no personal data, reply `Result: pass` and say "not applicable".
