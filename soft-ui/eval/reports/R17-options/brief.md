# R17 · Choose a hosting platform

The team must choose where to host the new API. `options.json` holds 4 options, each
with a score from 1 to 5 against each criterion, plus pros and cons. `criteria.json`
holds the criteria and their weights. `costs.csv` holds the projected monthly cost of
each option for the next 12 months.

Reader: the CTO, who decides this week. They need to compare the options side by side,
see the weighted ranking and how close it is, see how the costs differ over time, and
read the main pros and cons of each option.
