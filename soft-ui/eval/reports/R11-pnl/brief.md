# R11 · Profit and loss against budget

`pnl.csv` holds the monthly profit and loss of "Kōwhai Studio", a small design firm, for
July to December 2026. Each row is one account in one month, with the actual and the
budget amount in NZD. Each account belongs to a section: Revenue, Cost of sales, or
Operating expenses.

Reader: the owner and their accountant at the half-year review. They need the net profit
for the half year against budget, how the profit is built up from revenue down to net,
which accounts are over or under budget and by how much, and the monthly trend.
