# R2 · Vet scorecard

/vet compared the baseline and a candidate rewrite of the skill "quiz-plan". `scores.json`
holds the score of each rubric item for each version, `waves.csv` holds the overall score
after each wave of runs, and `run.json` holds the facts of the run.

Reader: the skill author. They need to know if the candidate is better or worse, which
rubric items moved and by how much, whether the scores settled, and whether to merge.
