The orders database, in one page

Eight tables. Everything hangs off orders: a customer places an order, the order has line items, payments and discounts. Read the map first, then the size table, then the detail of the table you need.

The huborders is the centre. Four tables point at it: order_items, payments, order_discounts, and it points out to customers and addresses.
The big onesorder_items is 1.2 GB with 1.18 M rows. orders (410 MB) and payments (350 MB) follow. Together they hold about 97% of the data.
ConventionsKeys are uuid, except discount_codes.code (text). Money is integer cents (*_cents). Status columns use enums: order_status, payment_status.
Watch fororder_discounts has no primary key in the schema. orders.address_id and orders.placed_at can be null, so not every order has an address or a placed time.
