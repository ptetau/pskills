Door sensor node: hardware and alarm logic

A battery-powered nRF52840 node with three sensors, a buzzer, a status LED and BLE. The page has two parts: the block diagram with its buses and power domains, and the full truth table of the alarm logic. The table marks the three input cases that raise the alarm in red.

Block diagram

Each shaded area is one voltage domain. Lines show power rails, GPIO, the I2C bus and the RF path. The I2C bus is the only signal that crosses domains (1V8 to 3V3). It goes through a level shifter, marked LS.
